About Sri Lankan Train Journeys

Sri Lanka's railway journey is a wonderful way to explore the beauty of the island. From the busy streets of Colombo to the green hills of Kandy, Ella and Nuwara Eliya, passengers can enjoy beautiful scenery, mountains, tea plantations and villages along the way. Train journeys in Sri Lanka are popular with both local passengers and tourists.
